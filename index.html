About

M-A Craft™ was created by Engineering Softcodes ™ to bring the power of 3D AI generation to everyone—for free. This platform allows users to create, preview, and export 3D assets with no restrictions.

Features

  • 🔷 AI-generated 3D models (characters, props, environments)
  • 🔶 Style selection: low poly, realistic, voxel, etc.
  • ⚙️ Export in multiple formats (.glb, .obj, .fbx, etc.)
  • 🎨 Integrated 3D viewer (coming soon!)
  • 🆓 100% Free, forever. No locked features. No pro version.

What Can It Do?

  • Generate voxel-based characters for Minecraft-style games
  • Create realistic environments for cutscenes and renders
  • Auto-generate sci-fi props, low poly assets, and vehicles
  • Use models directly in Unity, Unreal, or Blender
